How referral rewards work

1. Share your referral link.

2. Your friend registers using your link.

3. Your friend pays the KSh 350 activation fee.

4. Admin verifies the payment and activates the account.

5. KSh 105 is credited to your referral wallet.

You need at least 5 successful activated referrals before withdrawing referral rewards. Five successful referrals earn KSh 525. Task-wallet withdrawals are not affected.
